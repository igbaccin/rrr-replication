# Deposited results

`corrected/` is the analytical and exhibit bundle for the paper's reported
profile, vlm.2.5 materialized on the vlm.2.4 generation. Its manifest records
941 writer-stage attempts, of which 897 produced reviews and 44 ended in
writer failure, together with 300 independent baselines and 1,184 pre-writer
terminal outcomes.

## Reported local battery

`local_battery_vlm_2_5/` preserves the reported local-model battery. It
contains 941 writer-stage attempts generated by vlm.2.4 on 30 October 2026 and
finished by the vlm.2.5 quotation verifier, a deterministic pass that changed
three of the 897 produced reviews.

The deposited outcomes are:

- 889 successful runs, all free of E1 through E5
- 8 checker-flagged runs, all in the small-model ladder
- 44 writer failures
- 897 produced review files

The reference model, Mistral Small 24B, released 631 reviews in 635 attempts
across the main, ablation, high-floor, prompt-pressure, topic-transfer, and
corpus-size conditions, and all 631 are free of E1 through E5 under the
automated checker. The main condition contains 100 released reviews in 100
attempts.

`summary.json`, `per_run.csv`, and `conditions/*.json` contain the deposited
counts. `reviews_raw/` contains the 897 generated review files,
`reviews_pre_materialization/` the three reviews as they stood before the
vlm.2.5 pass, and `materialization.json` the record of that pass.
`run_records/phase_a_main/run_001/` deposits the complete record of the
run that the paper follows as its running example. `REVIEWS_README.md`
explains the reproduction and denominator rules.

## Previously accepted battery

`local_battery_vlm_2_1/` preserves the vlm.2.1 generation that the paper
reported when it was accepted on 27 October 2026. Its 941 attempts produced
907 reviews, of which 860 were free of E1 through E5. The final version of the
paper replaced these results after a rendering defect in the local writer was
found and corrected; `local_battery_vlm_2_1/REVIEWS_README.md` documents that
generation and its normalization records.

## Paper artifacts

`corrected/` contains the analytical records, tables, and figures for the
reported profile. It records 35,384 parsed citations across 897 produced
reviews, 44 writer failures, and the 100-clean Phase A result. The model table
and graph account for all 105 attempted runs per ladder model, including
pre-writer outcomes and writer failures.

## Reproduction material

`rebuild_inputs/pre_correction_snapshot/` contains the label and baseline
inputs required by the exhibit builder.

`external_comparisons/claude_code/` contains the completed direct Claude Code
comparisons. `external_comparisons/notebooklm/` contains five completed
NotebookLM records, the scoring sheet, and the separate five-claim page check.
