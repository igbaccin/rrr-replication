The debate is not merely about the extent of institutional impact but also about the mechanisms through which institutions shape economic outcomes. Some scholars highlight the importance of institutional frameworks in fostering economic development, while others caution against overgeneralizing the relationship between institutions and growth, emphasizing the role of historical and contextual factors. The tension lies in whether institutional structures are inherently conducive to growth or if they are contingent on specific conditions. The evidence suggests that institutions play a significant role in shaping economic outcomes, but the nature of this role is nuanced and context-dependent.
